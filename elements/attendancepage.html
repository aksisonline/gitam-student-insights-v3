Process to be followed for QR Attendance

1. App Installation & Registration
  • While installing, the app captures your device ID and user ID, linking them together.
2. Connect to GITAM WiFi
  • Attendance works only when connected to the official GITAM WiFi network.
3. QR Code Scanning
  • Open the app and scan the classroom QR code to mark attendance.
4. Auto-Refresh
  • The system updates attendance within 15 seconds.
5. Device Change Process
  • To use a new device, the old one must be unblocked.
  • Contact faculty or the CATS team for assistance.
6. Platform Availability
  • The app is supported on both iOS and Android.
